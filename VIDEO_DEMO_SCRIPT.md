## 🛠️ TIPS FOR RECORDING & EDITING YOUR DEMO VIDEO

1. **Recording Tool**: Use **OBS Studio** or **Loom** in 1080p (1920x1080) at 60fps.
2. **Audio**: Use a clear USB microphone; speak with an energetic, authoritative, and visionary tone.
3. **Pacing**: Move your mouse deliberately; pause for 1 second after clicking buttons to let viewers absorb the smooth Claymorphism animations.
4. **Editing Software**: CapCut, Premiere Pro, or DaVinci Resolve (add subtle background cinematic tech music at -22dB volume).
